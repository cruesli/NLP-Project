ingredients: |-
  Marinade and chicken:
  - 600g chicken thighs, boneless, sliced into 1cm strips
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil
  - Juice of 2 limes
  - 4 garlic cloves, minced
  - 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  - 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
  - 1 teaspoon chili powder
  - 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 1 teaspoon black pepper

  Peppers and onions:
  - 1 red bell pepper, sliced
  - 1 yellow bell pepper, sliced
  - 1 green bell pepper, sliced
  - 2 large onions, sliced
  - 2 tablespoons neutral oil

  To serve:
  - 8 flour tortillas, warmed
  - Sour cream
  - Guacamole
  - Lime wedges
  - Fresh cilantro
---
1. Marinate: Combine 2 tablespoons olive oil, juice of 2 limes, 4 minced garlic cloves, 2 teaspoons cumin, 2 teaspoons smoked paprika, 1 teaspoon chili powder, 1 teaspoon oregano, 1 teaspoon salt and 1 teaspoon black pepper in a bowl. Toss 600g sliced chicken thighs through. Marinate for 15 minutes while you prep the vegetables.

2. Cook peppers and onions: Heat 1 tablespoon neutral oil in a large cast-iron pan over high heat. Add 2 sliced onions and all 3 sliced peppers. Cook for 6 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and charred at the edges. Move to a plate.

3. Cook chicken: Add the remaining 1 tablespoon oil to the same pan. Spread the chicken in a single layer and cook undisturbed for 3 minutes to develop a crust. Toss and cook for another 3 minutes until cooked through.

4. Combine: Return the peppers and onions to the pan and toss with the chicken for 1 minute to bring everything together and reheat.

5. Serve: Pile onto a hot platter. Serve with warm tortillas, sour cream, guacamole, lime wedges and fresh cilantro, and let everyone build their own.

Cooking the peppers and chicken separately keeps the chicken from steaming and the peppers from going limp. A cast-iron pan and high heat give the proper char that defines fajitas. Serve the platter still sizzling — it's part of the experience.
