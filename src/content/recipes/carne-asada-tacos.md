ingredients: |-
  Carne asada:
  - 800g skirt or flank steak
  - Juice of 2 limes
  - Juice of 1 orange
  - 4 garlic cloves, grated
  - 1 small handful fresh cilantro, chopped
  - 2 tablespoons olive oil
  - 2 teaspoons ground cumin
  - 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 1 teaspoon black pepper

  To serve:
  - 12 small corn tortillas
  - 1 white onion, finely diced
  - 1 small handful fresh cilantro, chopped
  - 2 limes, cut into wedges
  - Salsa verde or salsa roja
---
1. Marinate: Whisk all marinade ingredients in a bowl. Add 800g skirt steak, turn to coat, cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, up to 6.

2. Heat the grill: Get a grill, griddle pan or cast-iron pan as hot as it will go — almost smoking. Wipe excess marinade off the steak.

3. Grill: Cook the steak for 3 minutes on the first side without moving it, then 2 to 3 minutes on the second side for medium-rare. The exterior should be deeply charred.

4. Rest: Move to a board and rest for 10 minutes. Essential — slicing early loses all the juices.

5. Warm tortillas: Char 12 corn tortillas directly over a flame for 10 seconds per side, or in a dry pan, until pliable and slightly blistered. Stack and wrap in a clean tea towel to keep warm.

6. Slice: Slice the steak against the grain into 5mm strips, then chop into bite-sized pieces.

7. Build: Pile chopped steak onto each tortilla. Top with diced white onion, cilantro and a squeeze of lime. Pass the salsa at the table.

Skirt steak has the best flavour, but flank works too. Slicing against the grain is what makes the difference between tender and chewy — find the direction the muscle fibres run and cut perpendicular to them. Double up the tortillas if they tear easily; that's how it's done in most taquerías.
