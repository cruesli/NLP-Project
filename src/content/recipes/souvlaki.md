ingredients: |-
  Souvlaki:
  - 800g pork shoulder, cut into 3cm cubes
  - 4 tablespoons olive oil
  - Juice of 1 lemon
  - 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  - 4 garlic cloves, grated
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - 1 teaspoon black pepper

  To serve:
  - 4 pita breads, warmed
  - 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  - 2 tomatoes, sliced
  - Tzatziki
  - Lemon wedges
---
1. Marinate: Whisk 4 tablespoons olive oil, the juice of 1 lemon, 2 teaspoons dried oregano, 4 grated garlic cloves, 1 teaspoon salt and 1 teaspoon black pepper in a bowl. Toss 800g pork cubes through the marinade. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour, up to 8.

2. Skewer: If using bamboo skewers, soak them in water for 30 minutes. Thread the pork tightly onto the skewers, packing the cubes against each other.

3. Grill: Heat a grill, griddle pan or barbecue to high. Grill the skewers for 12 to 15 minutes, turning every few minutes, until charred outside and just cooked through.

4. Rest: Let rest on a board for 5 minutes — non-negotiable.

5. Serve: Wrap the meat in warm pita with red onion, tomato, a generous spoon of tzatziki and a squeeze of lemon.

Pork shoulder beats lean cuts every time — the fat keeps the meat juicy over high heat. Pack the cubes tightly on the skewer (touching, not spaced) for a juicy interior with a charred crust. Spaced cubes cook to dry, sad little blocks.
