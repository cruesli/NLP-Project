ingredients: |-
  - 4 medium potatoes, sliced into 5mm rounds
  - 3 zucchini, sliced into 5mm rounds
  - 4 tomatoes, sliced
  - 200g passata or grated tomato
  - 2 red onions, thinly sliced
  - 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  - 100ml olive oil
  - 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  - 1 small handful fresh parsley, chopped
  - Salt and black pepper
  - Crumbled feta, to serve (optional)
---
1. Preheat: Heat oven to 180°C.

2. Mix: In a large bowl, toss 4 sliced potatoes, 3 sliced zucchini, 2 sliced red onions and 4 sliced garlic cloves with 100ml olive oil, 2 teaspoons oregano, half the parsley and a generous amount of salt and black pepper.

3. Layer: Tip into a large baking dish. Lay 4 sliced tomatoes on top. Pour 200g passata over and around the vegetables. The dish should look full and a little wet.

4. Roast: Cover with foil and bake for 45 minutes. Remove the foil and bake for another 30 minutes, until the potatoes are tender, the edges are caramelised and most of the liquid has reduced.

5. Finish: Scatter with the rest of the parsley and crumbled feta if using. Let rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Briam improves as it sits, make it earlier in the day or even the day before. Serve warm or at room temperature with crusty bread to mop up the oil.
