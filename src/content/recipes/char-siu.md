ingredients: |-
  - 1kg pork shoulder, cut into 4cm-thick strips

  Marinade:
  - 3 tablespoons hoisin sauce
  - 2 tablespoons light soy sauce
  - 1 tablespoon dark soy sauce
  - 2 tablespoons Shaoxing wine
  - 2 cubes red fermented bean curd, plus 1 tablespoon of its liquid
  - 2 tablespoons honey
  - 2 tablespoons sugar
  - 1 teaspoon five-spice powder
  - 4 garlic cloves, grated
  - 1 teaspoon white pepper

  Glaze:
  - 3 tablespoons honey
  - 2 tablespoons reserved marinade
---
1. Marinate: Whisk all marinade ingredients in a bowl. Reserve 2 tablespoons for the glaze. Toss 1kg pork shoulder strips in the rest, cover and refrigerate for at least 8 hours, ideally overnight.

2. Preheat: Heat oven to 220°C with a rack in the middle. Place a tray of water on the bottom shelf — it keeps the pork moist and catches drips.

3. First roast: Lay the pork on a rack over a foil-lined tray. Roast for 25 minutes, turning halfway.

4. Glaze: Mix 3 tablespoons honey with 2 tablespoons reserved marinade. Brush the pork generously, flip, and brush again.

5. Char: Return to the oven and roast for another 15 minutes, brushing with glaze every 5 minutes, until the edges are deeply caramelised and slightly charred. Finish under a hot grill for 2 minutes if needed.

6. Rest: Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing against the grain into thin pieces.

Pork shoulder with a generous mix of fat and meat is essential — lean cuts dry out under the high heat. The fermented red bean curd is what gives the savoury depth and natural pink colour; food colouring is optional and only for the classic restaurant look. Excellent in fried rice, bao buns, or sliced over rice with steamed greens and a soft-boiled egg.
