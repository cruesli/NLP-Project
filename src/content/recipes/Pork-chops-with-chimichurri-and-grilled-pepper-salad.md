  Pork chops:
  - 400g pork shoulder
  - 6g Salt
  - Pepper to taste

  Grilled pepper salad:
  - 2 Large bell peppers
  - 100g corn salad
  - 10g pine nuts
  - 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
  - 1 tsp Balsamic vinegar
  - Salt and pepper

  Chimichurri:
  - 1/2 cup Parsley, finely chopped
  - 1 red chilli, finely chopped
  - 1 clove garlic, finely chopped
  - 1/4 cup olive oil
  - pinch of oregano
  - 1 tsp Balsamic vinegar
  - Salt and pepper

Pork chops (20 min):

1. Ideally salt the pork at least 1 hour before cooking. Pepper before cooking.

2. Heat up grill or pan. Depending on if you are using 2 chops or one thick one adjust the temperature up or down. Either way the heat should be high.

3. Sear the meat until nicely coloured on both sides, and reaching an internal temp of 55 deg (medium rare).

4. Let meat rest for 5-10 minutes.

Grilled Bell pepper salad (30-40 min):

1. Char the bell peppers on either a grill or under the broiler in an oven until completely black.

2. Put the peppers in a container and cover them to let them steam in their own heat for about 15 mins

3. While the peppers are steaming. Wash the salad if needed, and thinly slice the onion.

4. Ideally toast the pine nuts until lightly browned.

5. After 15 mins, peel the skin of the peppers, and deseed them. Then slice into 1cm thick strips.

6. Toss everything together and add the balsamic vinegar, salt and pepper.

Chimichurri (5 min):

1. Finely chop the parsley, chilli and garlic.

2. Add to a bowl and add the oregano, salt and pepper, and stir together.

3. Add oil and balsamic vinegar and stir well.

4. Can be eaten right away, but gets better the longer it sits. So make ahead of time if possible.
