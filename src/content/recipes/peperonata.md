ingredients: |-
  - 4 sweet peppers, sliced into strips
  - 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  - 3 garlic cloves, sliced
  - 400g ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped
  - 2 tablespoons capers
  - 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar
  - 1 teaspoon sugar
  - 3 tablespoons olive oil
  - 1 small handful fresh basil
---

1. Cook peppers: Add 4 mixed sweet peppers, sliced and cook for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until they begin to collapse and caramelise.

2. Onion and Garlic: Thinly slice the red onion and add the red onion, and cook for about 8 minutes until soft. Add the sliced garlic cloves, and cook for another 2 minutes.

3. Finish: Add 3 ripe tomatoes, roughly chopped, 2 tablespoons capers, 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar and 1 teaspoon sugar. Season well and cook for another 15 minutes on low heat until jammy and thick.

4. Season: Tear in 1 small handful fresh basil and adjust seasoning. Add a little more vinegar if it needs brightness.

This is better made ahead, it improves significantly as it sits. Make it first and let it come to room temperature before serving. Can be served warm or at room temperature.
