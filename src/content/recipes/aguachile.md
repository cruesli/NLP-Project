ingredients: |-
  - 500g raw shrimp, peeled, deveined and butterflied
  - Juice of 8 limes (about 200ml)
  - 3 serrano chiles, stems removed
  - 1 small handful fresh cilantro
  - 2 garlic cloves
  - 1 cucumber, half diced and half thinly sliced
  - 1 red onion, thinly sliced
  - 1 avocado, sliced
  - 1 teaspoon salt
  - Tostadas or tortilla chips, to serve
---
1. Cure: Lay 500g shrimp in a single layer on a shallow dish. Pour the juice of 4 limes over them and toss. Let cure for 10 minutes — they should turn opaque pink at the edges but stay a little translucent in the middle.

2. Soak onion: While the shrimp cures, soak 1 thinly sliced red onion in cold water for 10 minutes to soften its bite. Drain well.

3. Make the aguachile: Blend the juice of the remaining 4 limes, 3 serrano chiles, 1 small handful cilantro, 2 garlic cloves, half the cucumber (diced) and 1 teaspoon salt until completely smooth and a vivid green.

4. Combine: Drain the shrimp and discard the curing liquid. Pour the green aguachile over the shrimp and toss gently. Let sit for 5 minutes.

5. Plate: Spoon the shrimp and sauce onto a wide plate. Top with the sliced cucumber, drained red onion and 1 sliced avocado. Serve immediately with tostadas.

Use the freshest shrimp you can find — this is essentially raw, so quality matters. The lime juice "cooks" the shrimp through acidulation, but don't cure them too long or they turn rubbery. Adjust chile to taste; remove the seeds and ribs for less heat. This dish does not keep — make it just before serving.
