ingredients: |-
  - 4 chicken thighs, boneless, skin on (about 500g)
  - 1 teaspoon neutral oil
  - 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  - 3 tablespoons mirin
  - 3 tablespoons sake
  - 1 tablespoon sugar
  - Steamed rice, to serve
  - Steamed greens, to serve
  - Toasted sesame seeds, to serve
---
1. Prepare chicken: Pat 4 chicken thighs dry with paper towel. Score the thicker parts of the meat lightly with a knife so they cook evenly. Don't season — the sauce is salty enough.

2. Mix sauce: Stir 3 tablespoons soy sauce, 3 tablespoons mirin, 3 tablespoons sake and 1 tablespoon sugar in a small bowl until the sugar dissolves.

3. Sear skin: Heat 1 teaspoon neutral oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Place the chicken skin-side down and cook for 8 minutes without moving it — the skin should turn deep golden and crisp. Drain off the rendered fat from the pan.

4. Flip and cook: Flip the chicken and cook for another 4 minutes until almost cooked through.

5. Glaze: Pour in the sauce. Bring to a bubble and tilt the pan to baste the chicken constantly. Cook for 3 minutes, spooning sauce over the chicken, until the sauce reduces to a glossy syrup that coats the meat.

6. Rest and slice: Move to a board and rest for 3 minutes. Slice into 1cm strips.

7. Serve: Lay the sliced chicken over hot rice. Spoon the pan glaze over the top. Serve with steamed greens and a sprinkle of sesame seeds.

Authentic teriyaki has only four ingredients in the sauce — no garlic, no ginger, no cornstarch. The glaze comes from naturally reducing the sauce to a syrup, not thickening with starch. Skin-on thighs are essential — the rendered fat carries the flavour and the crisp skin gives texture against the soft glaze.
