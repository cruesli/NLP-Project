prepTimeMinutes: 20
cookTimeMinutes: 10
image: /images/recipes/gongbao-chicken.png
ingredients: |-
  Chicken and marinade:
  - 500g chicken thighs, boneless, skinless, cut into 2cm cubes
  - 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine
  - 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
  - 1 teaspoon cornstarch

  Sauce:
  - 2 tablespoons Chinkiang black vinegar
  - 1 tablespoon light soy sauce
  - 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine
  - 2 teaspoons sugar
  - 1 teaspoon cornstarch
  - 2 tablespoons water

  Stir-fry:
  - 12 dried Sichuan chiles, snipped, seeds shaken out
  - 2 teaspoons whole Sichuan peppercorns
  - 4 garlic cloves, sliced
  - 1 thumb ginger, sliced
  - 4 spring onions, white parts only, cut into 2cm pieces
  - 80g unsalted roasted peanuts
  - 3 tablespoons neutral oil
---
1. Marinate: Toss 500g chicken cubes with 1 tablespoon Shaoxing wine, 1 tablespoon light soy and 1 teaspoon cornstarch. Set aside for 15 minutes.

2. Mix sauce: Combine all sauce ingredients in a small bowl and stir until the cornstarch dissolves. Set within reach of the stove.

3. Bloom aromatics: Heat a wok over high heat until smoking. Add 3 tablespoons neutral oil, then immediately add 12 dried chiles and 2 teaspoons Sichuan peppercorns. Toss for 20 seconds until fragrant — don't let them burn.

4. Stir-fry chicken: Add the chicken in a single layer and let it sear for 30 seconds before stirring. Cook for 3 minutes until just cooked through.

5. Aromatics: Add 4 sliced garlic cloves, 1 thumb sliced ginger and 4 spring onion whites. Toss for 30 seconds.

6. Sauce: Stir the sauce again to lift the cornstarch off the bottom, then pour it into the wok. Toss for 30 seconds until glossy and the sauce coats every piece.

7. Finish: Off the heat, fold in 80g peanuts. Serve immediately with steamed rice.

The peanuts go in last to keep them crunchy. Authentic gongbao chicken is deep brown and glossy, not red — the colour comes from soy and vinegar, not chili oil. The Sichuan peppercorns provide the signature numbing tingle (麻); skip them and it's just a stir-fry. Don't eat the dried chiles unless you mean it.
