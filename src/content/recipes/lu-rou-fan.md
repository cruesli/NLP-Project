servings: 4
prepTimeMinutes: 20
cookTimeMinutes: 90
image: /images/recipes/lu-rou-fan.png
ingredients: |-
  - 600g pork belly, skin on, diced into 1cm pieces
  - 6 shallots, thinly sliced
  - 4 garlic cloves, minced
  - 1 thumb ginger, sliced
  - 4 tablespoons dark soy sauce
  - 2 tablespoons light soy sauce
  - 3 tablespoons Shaoxing rice wine
  - 2 tablespoons rock sugar
  - 1 teaspoon five-spice powder
  - 1 star anise
  - 1 cinnamon stick
  - 4 dried shiitake mushrooms, soaked and diced
  - 4 soft-boiled eggs, peeled
  - 500ml water or pork stock
  - Steamed rice, to serve
  - Pickled greens, to serve
---
1. Render: Heat a heavy-based pot over medium heat. Add 600g pork belly, diced and cook for 10 minutes, stirring, until the fat renders out and the pork is golden. Drain off most of the fat, leaving 2 tablespoons in the pot.

2. Fry shallots: Add 6 shallots, sliced and cook for 8 minutes until deeply golden and crisp. Add 4 garlic cloves, minced and 1 thumb ginger, sliced and cook for another 1 minute.

3. Caramelise: Push everything to the side. Add 2 tablespoons rock sugar to the centre of the pot and let it melt and turn amber, about 2 minutes. Stir to coat the pork.

4. Build the braise: Add 3 tablespoons Shaoxing wine and let it bubble. Pour in 4 tablespoons dark soy sauce, 2 tablespoons light soy sauce, 1 teaspoon five-spice powder, 1 star anise, 1 cinnamon stick, 4 diced shiitake mushrooms and 500ml water. Bring to a simmer.

5. Simmer: Cover partially and cook on low for 75 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the pork is silky and the sauce is glossy and reduced.

6. Add eggs: Add 4 soft-boiled eggs in the last 15 minutes so they soak up the braising liquid and turn a deep mahogany.

7. Serve: Spoon generously over hot steamed rice with an egg on top and pickled greens on the side.

Much better the next day, after the flavours settle and the fat firms slightly. Make a big batch and freeze portions. The braising liquid is gold — save any leftover and use it as a base for noodles or to braise more eggs.
